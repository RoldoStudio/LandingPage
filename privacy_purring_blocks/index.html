Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 21, 2026

Introduction

Welcome to PurringBlocks. This Privacy Policy explains how Roldo Studio ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, discloses, and safeguards your information when you play our mobile game, PurringBlocks (the "App"), also referred to as "BlockMerge" in certain web versions. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data.

Because Roldo Studio is based in Spain, we comply with the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the Spanish Organic Law on Data Protection (LOPDGDD).

Data We Collect

We aim for "Data Minimization," meaning we only collect what is strictly necessary:

  • Login Data: If you sign in via Google Play Games or Apple Game Center, we receive a unique user ID and your profile name to manage your account and save progress.
  • Leaderboard Data: Your username and score are processed to display your ranking on the public leaderboards.
  • Technical Identifiers: We (and our partners) may collect device-specific information like your IP address and Advertising IDs (IDFA for iOS / AAID for Android).

Third-Party Services

Our game uses third-party SDKs that may collect data independently under their own privacy policies:

  • Google AdMob: Used to serve advertisements. They may use device identifiers to personalize ads.
  • Unity Ads: Used for video and interstitial ads.
  • Google Play / Apple Game Center: Used for authentication and leaderboard services.

Note: To comply with EU law, we use a Consent Management Platform (CMP). You can change your ad preferences or withdraw consent for personalized ads at any time in the game's settings.

Legal Basis for Processing

Under GDPR, we process your data based on:

  • Contractual Necessity: To provide the login and leaderboard features you requested.
  • Consent: For personalized advertising (provided via the in-game pop-up).
  • Legitimate Interest: For basic game analytics and fraud prevention.

Your Rights (GDPR)

Since you are playing a game governed by EU law, you have the right to:

  • Access the data we have about you.
  • Rectify any incorrect data.
  • Delete your data ("Right to be forgotten").
  • Object to processing or withdraw consent.

To exercise these rights, please contact us at: block.merge.dev@gmail.com.

Children's Privacy

In Spain, the age of digital consent is 14. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under this age without parental consent. If we discover we have inadvertently collected such data, we will delete it immediately.

Data Transfers

Data may be transferred to servers located outside the European Economic Area (EEA), specifically to the US (Google/Apple/Unity). These companies use Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) to ensure your data remains protected.

Contact Us

Introduction

Welcome to Purring Blocks. This Privacy Policy explains how Roldo Studios ("we", "us" or "our") collects, uses, shares and protects your information when you play our mobile game Purring Blocks (the "App"), also called "BlockMerge" in some web versions.

Roldo Studios is based in Spain, so we comply with the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the Spanish Organic Law on Data Protection (LOPDGDD).

Data We Collect

We only collect what we need to run the game, keep your progress and fund it through optional ads:

  • Account and progress data: a guest account is created automatically when you first play. If you sign in with Google Play Games, Sign in with Apple or Apple Game Center, we receive a unique user ID and your profile name. We use them to save your progress (levels, stars, coins, items) and sync it across devices.
  • Leaderboard data: your username and score, shown on the public leaderboards.
  • Device and technical data: device model, operating system, language, country, IP address and, depending on your choices, your advertising ID (AAID on Android, IDFA on iOS).
  • Gameplay events: if you agree to measurement (see Your choices), we record events such as the first launch, levels completed, tutorial completed and in-app purchases.
  • Advertising data: which ads were shown to you and the revenue each impression earned.
  • Purchases: if you buy something in the game, the store (Google Play or the App Store) tells us what was bought and gives us a transaction ID so we can deliver it and verify it is genuine. We never receive your payment details.

Third-Party Services

The game uses the following third-party services. They may process data under their own privacy policies:

  • AppLovin MAX (ad mediation) and the ad networks it works with. All ads in the game are optional rewarded videos you choose to watch. These partners may use your advertising ID to show personalized ads, but only if you consent.
    • Google AdMob
    • Meta Audience Network
    • Unity Ads
    • Mintegral
    • BidMachine
    • Liftoff (Vungle)
    • InMobi
    • DT Exchange (Digital Turbine)
    • Pangle (ByteDance)
  • AppsFlyer (attribution and measurement): only if you answer "Yes" to the in-game privacy question. AppsFlyer measures which advertising campaign brought you to the game, the gameplay events listed above and ad revenue. It shares this information with our advertising partners, such as Meta, so we can measure and improve our ads. If you answer "No", nothing is sent to AppsFlyer, not even the installation.
  • Google Play and the Apple App Store: in-app purchases. Payments are handled entirely by the store.
  • Google Play Games, Sign in with Apple and Apple Game Center: sign-in, progress sync and leaderboards.

Your Choices

  • In-game privacy question: when you first launch the game, we ask whether we may use your device's data to measure our ads and show you more relevant ones. The game is exactly the same either way.
  • EEA and UK: you are also shown our consent management platform (Google's consent form, through AppLovin MAX), where you can choose how ad partners may use your data.
  • iOS: the system asks your permission before any app may use your advertising ID (App Tracking Transparency). You can change this in your device settings.
  • Android: you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device's Google settings.

Change your answer at any time: open Settings in the game and tap the shield button.

Google API Services User Data Policy (Google User Data Disclosure)

To comply with the Google API Services User Data Policy, we clearly disclose how our app uses your Google user data.

1. Data accessed

When you use Google Sign-In or Google Play Games Services in Purring Blocks, we request access only to basic profile information: your Google user ID and email address. We do not access your contacts, files or any other data associated with your Google account.

2. Data usage and purpose

We use this information only to:

  • Authenticate you: securely identify you as a unique player.
  • Sync your progress: save, recover and synchronize your progress across devices through our servers.

3. Data storage and sharing

Your Google user ID is transmitted securely and stored on our servers to keep your saved progress. We do not share, sell, trade or transfer your Google user data to third parties, advertisers or other organizations.

Legal Basis for Processing

Under the GDPR, we process your data on the following bases:

  • Contract (providing the game): your account, saved progress, leaderboards and the delivery of purchases.
  • Consent: ad measurement and attribution (AppsFlyer) and personalized advertising. You can withdraw consent at any time in Settings; withdrawing does not affect processing that happened before.
  • Legitimate interest: security, fraud prevention (for example, checking that purchases are genuine) and serving non-personalized ads.

Data Retention

We keep your account and progress data while your account exists. If you ask us to delete your account, we delete your data from our servers within 30 days, unless the law requires us to keep it longer (for example, purchase records). Third-party partners keep data according to their own policies.

Your Rights (GDPR)

You have the right to:

  • Access the data we hold about you.
  • Correct inaccurate data.
  • Delete your data and your account (the "right to be forgotten").
  • Object to or restrict processing.
  • Data portability.
  • Withdraw your consent at any time.
  • Complain to the Spanish Data Protection Agency (Agencia Española de Protección de Datos, www.aepd.es).

To exercise any of these rights, including deleting your account, email us at info@roldostudios.com from the address you want us to reply to, and tell us your in-game user ID if you can.

Children's Privacy

The game is not directed at children under 14, the age of digital consent in Spain. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under this age without parental consent. If we learn that we have done so, we will delete it immediately.

International Data Transfers

Some of our partners process data outside the European Economic Area, including in the United States (Google, Apple, Unity, AppLovin, Meta, InMobi, Liftoff, Digital Turbine), Israel (AppsFlyer), Singapore and China (Pangle/ByteDance, Mintegral). These transfers are protected by an adequacy decision of the European Commission (such as the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or the decision on Israel) or by Standard Contractual Clauses.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy when the game or our partners change. We will post the new version on this page with a new "Last updated" date. If the changes are significant, we will let you know in the game.
